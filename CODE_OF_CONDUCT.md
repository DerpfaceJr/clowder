# Code of Conduct

Clowder follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text is at that link and is the authoritative version.

## Summary

- Be respectful and constructive, in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Assume good faith, and say clearly when something is a problem.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, or publishing someone's private information.
- Keep discussion on the project. Disagreement is fine; hostility is not.
- Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions that break these rules, and may restrict or ban participants who repeatedly do.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns through GitHub private vulnerability reporting on this repository, as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). A public contact address will be published on the website when it is set up.
